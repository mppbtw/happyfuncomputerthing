Uses of Computing in Education

Virtual Classrooms

Students are able to study from anywhere, at any time. This means students who may have other unavoidable commitments, such as sports are still able to access education remotely. It also means that scheduling conflicts can be easily resolved, as students are able to miss in-person lessons and still catch up on any content they may have missed. Furthermore, homework can be easily set and tracked by teachers with written work being able to be submitted and marked remotely with clear deadlines, or online quizzes being provided. It also allows all students to work at their own pace; when learning in-person, there is no way to change the speed at which something is being taught, while in a virtual classroom, all students can decide themselves how quickly they want to learn.

Assistive Technologies

Computing has greatly improved the learning experience for students who may have disabilites or difficulties accessing some parts of education. Software such as text-to-speech or speech-to-text software means students who may find reading and writing difficult or slow are able to access education at the same level as students who may not have these difficulties.

Administrative Efficiency

Many administrative tasks in education have been made much easier by computing. For example, even just a teacher taking a register is something that would have to have been done on paper not that long ago, meaning someone would then have to read through the paper and calculate and record each student's attendance individually. Nowadays, teachers will complete the register on a computer system, which will then automatically calculate their attendance and communicate it to their parents. Another example of this is the fact that many teachers will now use AI to help them write their slides. Although this is a contentious topic, some may argue that this means they have more time for feedback and for working with students directly.

Interactive Learning

Websites such as Kahoot and Blooket have become increasingly popular over the last few years, and have certainly helped students to stay engaged with their studies. Anything interactive, especially if there is a game component to it, will make it far easier for students to remain actively engaged with what they are learning.
